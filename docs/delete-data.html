BizStreak: Delete your data

Oct 5, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data connected to the BizStreak app.

Your habit data (stored on your phone)

BizStreak has no accounts and no server. Your habits and streak history are stored only on your device.

  1. To delete one habit and its history: open the habit in the app and delete it.
  2. To delete everything: uninstall BizStreak. All of its data is permanently removed from your phone.

Your purchase record (only if you bought BizStreak Pro)

If you bought a Pro plan, a limited purchase record (which plan you bought and its status) is processed by our subscription service, RevenueCat. To ask us to delete it:

  1. Email tenzitradingltd@gmail.com with the subject "BizStreak data deletion".
  2. Include the Google Play order number from your purchase receipt email, so we can find the record.
  3. We will delete the record within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your purchase record held by RevenueCat for BizStreak.
  • Not under our control: Google Play keeps its own payment and order records under Google's policies. Deleting our record does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play subscriptions.
