## Full description

BizStreak is a business habit tracker built for entrepreneurs and small
business owners. Track daily habits like stock checks, sales logging, and
invoice follow-ups, and build a streak that keeps you disciplined every
day.

Add the daily disciplines that matter to your business, check them off
each day, and watch your streak grow on a simple visual grid. Miss a day
and you'll see it — which is exactly why you won't want to.

• Track unlimited business habits (3 free, unlimited with Pro)
• See your current streak, best streak, and consistency over time
• Daily reminder so you never forget
• Share your streak with friends or your team
• 100% private — everything stays on your device, no account needed

Free to start. Upgrade anytime for unlimited habits.

## Positioning notes (why this copy, for future reference)
"Habit tracker" alone is a crowded term (Habitica, Streaks, etc.) that a
new app can't rank for. "Business habit tracker" has little competition —
it's in the title, subtitle, and the opening line above on purpose, aiming
to rank for that specific phrase rather than the broad one.

Ranking also depends on what happens after install, not just the text:
keep the star rating above 4.0 (ask happy users to rate, reply to bad
reviews), get a cluster of downloads close together at launch rather than
a slow trickle (the streak-share feature helps here), and ship updates
regularly rather than going stale.
