Pick the daily jobs that keep your business healthy — checking stock,
recording sales, following up unpaid invoices — tick them off, and watch
your streak grow.

START IN SECONDS

Free to start with up to 3 habits. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited habits.

## Positioning notes (why this copy, for future reference)
"Habit tracker" alone is a crowded term (Habitica, Streaks, etc.) that a
new app can't rank for. "Business habit tracker" has little competition —
it's in the title, short description and the opening line on purpose,
aiming to rank for that specific phrase rather than the broad one.

Ranking also depends on what happens after install, not just the text:
keep the star rating above 4.0 (ask happy users to rate, reply to bad
reviews), get a cluster of downloads close together at launch rather than
a slow trickle (the share cards help here), and ship updates regularly
rather than going stale.

Only describe features that are in the version users actually have.
Version 12 is the first to include everything above.
