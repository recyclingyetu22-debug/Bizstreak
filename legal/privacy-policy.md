# BizStreak Privacy Policy

_Sep 20, 2026_

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BizStreak ("the App") handles information
when you use it. BizStreak is a habit-tracking app for entrepreneurs and
small business owners, available on the App Store and Google Play.

This policy applies only to the BizStreak app itself, not to any other
product or service.

## Information We Collect

BizStreak is built to work entirely on your device. It does not require an
account, sign-up, or login.

- **Habit data** (the habits you create, their names, icons, colors, and
  the dates you mark them complete) is stored only in the app's local
  storage, on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server.
- We do not collect analytics, usage tracking, advertising identifiers, or
  crash reports at this time.
- We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

If a future update adds optional cloud sync, analytics, or crash
reporting, this policy will be updated first and, where required, your
consent will be requested before that feature is turned on.

## Permissions

BizStreak may ask for one permission:

- **Notifications** — used only to send you an optional daily reminder to
  check off your habits, at a time you choose in Settings. You can decline
  this permission and still use the full app; you just won't receive
  reminders.

BizStreak does not request any other device permissions.

## Subscriptions and Payments

BizStreak offers optional paid plans (monthly, yearly, or lifetime) that
unlock unlimited habits. If you choose to purchase one:

- The transaction is processed directly by the Apple App Store or Google
  Play, using the payment method already on file with your Apple ID or
  Google account.
- We never see or store your card number or other payment details — Apple
  and Google handle that entirely.
- We use **RevenueCat**, a subscription-management service, to confirm
  your purchase and keep your Pro features unlocked. RevenueCat receives
  limited purchase information (such as which plan you bought and its
  status) from Apple/Google on our behalf. RevenueCat's own privacy policy
  is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Children's Privacy

BizStreak is intended for business owners and is not directed at children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Since the app requires no account and stores data only on-device, we have
no way to identify or contact users of any age.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because all habit data lives only in the app's local storage on your
device:

- You can delete any habit, and all its history, at any time from within
  the app.
- Uninstalling BizStreak permanently deletes all of its data, since
  nothing is kept on any server.
- There is no account to close and no server-side copy of your data to
  request deletion of.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we
add a new feature that changes what information is collected. Any changes
will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use
of BizStreak after a change means you accept the updated policy.
